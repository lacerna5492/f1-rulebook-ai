Ask the 2026 F1 Rulebook

Type a question in plain English. The app finds the matching articles in the official FIA regulations (Section A, General, and Section B, Sporting) and an AI model writes the answer with the article it came from. All three AI models run entirely in your browser: no server, no account, nothing you type leaves your device.

How it works

  1. Find the rules. A sentence-embedding model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, 23 MB) compares your question with all 698 rule passages by meaning, blended with a keyword search that knows F1 slang (e.g. "DRS", "red flag", "Grand Prix").
  2. Rank them. A cross-encoder (ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2, 23 MB) reads your question together with each of the top 20 candidates and re-orders them.
  3. Write the answer. A small language model (LFM2-1.2B, 4-bit) reads the 3 best rules and writes a short answer that cites the article. It runs on your graphics card through WebGPU (Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop); without WebGPU the app still finds the matching rules.
  4. All local. The models, the rule text and the transformers.js runtime are part of this site. After the first load they are cached by your browser.
