Ask the 2026 F1 Sporting Regulations

Type a question in plain English. The app finds the matching articles in the official FIA rulebook and highlights the answer. Both AI models run entirely in your browser: no server, no account, nothing you type leaves your device.

How it works

  1. Find the rules. A small sentence-embedding model (Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, 23 MB) turns your question into a vector and compares it with every article of the rulebook to find the closest matches by meaning, not keywords.
  2. Find the answer. A question-answering model (Xenova/distilbert-base-cased-distilled-squad, 66 MB) reads the best matches and picks out the exact words that answer your question.
  3. All local. The models, the rule text and the transformers.js runtime are part of this site. After the first load they are cached by your browser.
